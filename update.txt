
ANALYTICS PAGE

Summary – Key Points

• Membership Data

◦ Break down members into Active, Pending, and other membership statuses.
◦ Classify members according to their membership level (Junior, Senior, Cadet, etc.).
◦ Identify which schools have the most members.
◦ Determine which membership levels have the highest and lowest population.
◦ Analyze the trend of new members and promotions.

• Member Classification

◦ Include members' levels, participation, and schools.
◦ Analyze promotions based on the age requirements for each level.
◦ Identify the number of members in each specific level.

• Badges and Achievements

◦ Break down members according to the badges they have earned.
◦ Identify which scouts have earned the most badges.
◦ Measure performance and achievements per membership level.
◦ Determine which areas have the highest and lowest achievements, 
    such as Arts and Culture or Leadership.

• Participation in Activities

◦ Track participation in activities such as coastal clean-ups and community activities.
◦ Compare active and inactive members.
◦ Identify members or schools with low participation.
◦ Use the data to create interventions or activities that can increase participation.

• Community Engagement

◦ Determine which members/schools contribute the most to community engagement.
◦ Analyze achievements such as Community Helper and other community-related badges.
◦ Compare community involvement across different schools or groups.

• Financial Data

◦ Do not show only the overall financial totals.
◦ Break down income and expenses by school, activity, or category.
◦ Identify which school or activity has the highest expenses.
◦ Compare spending between schools and activities.

• Data Visualization

◦ Turn the collected data into charts, graphs, and visualizations.
◦ Break down the data into specific categories so the overall 
    situation is easier to understand.

• Decision-Making

◦ The main purpose of the data is to support decision-making.
◦ Use the results to identify:
▪ Areas that need improvement
▪ Levels with low performance
▪ Schools with low participation
▪ Activities that need more support
▪ Areas that should receive more budget or priority
◦ The data should provide specific and actionable insights, not just total numbers.
• Events
◦ Break down the number of events by month, school, and type of activity.
◦ Example mentioned: events held in July and participation of different schools.

Main idea: The data should not only show totals. It should be broken 
down by membership status, level, school, participation, badges, achievements, 
finances, and activities so it can be properly visualized and used for 
better decision-making.
